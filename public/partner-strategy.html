04 · Scale play

Commerce Tools’ installed base

Goal

Keep CT’s enterprise accounts from leaving. viax closes the gap CT can’t close alone. It’s the biggest base we can reach, and their CEO has offered an intro to sales.

Approach

10 named accounts, not a generic pitch. Start with accounts coming up for renewal. Work with the team that sells to existing customers, since they’re easier to incent. Give up more margin early to prove the model. Executive sponsor plus a monthly account review.

Next steps

Follow-up email to CT’s CEO for the sales-team intro (next week) · Ex-CT sales contacts on what incentive would work (Brian) · Pick the 10 accounts and draft the revenue-share offer
